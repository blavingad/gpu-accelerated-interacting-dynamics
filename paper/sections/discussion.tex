\section{Discussion}
\subsection{What the results establish}
The two cases connect a computationally developed command table to printer deployment and a deposited-coordinate assessment. Complete computational coverage refers to the declared 644-context pool, derived from the supplied font and preceding/current-stroke policy. It does not imply coverage of every binary mask, arbitrary message boundary or unbounded emission history.

The fixed-command ablations show that omitting Coulomb or wake terms changes predictions for the developed tables and worsens agreement with the released measurements. Because the commands were developed with full physics, this is a conditional test of those designs. An alternative model given its own calibration and optimization would require a separate comparison.

The matched benchmark demonstrates accelerator benefit for the recorded endpoint workload. Compilation, first execution, output retention and dense-recording results qualify that measurement. No wall-clock comparison with a coupled CFD implementation or a physical-rig development campaign is supplied.

\subsection{Controller realization and inverse-design implications}
The controller representation is central to the methodology. Charges optimized independently for separate patterns can disagree at a shared dependency address. Jointly fitting those entries and replaying the integer waveform turns pattern-specific solutions into an implementable design. The coverage-size intervention demonstrates why characterization must include the intended print domain, while its unequal development costs prevent an equal-budget optimizer ranking.

The discrete schedule and continuous commands must be developed together. Reordering or moving guards changes charging history, near-neighbor flight and arrival timing. A structure can improve horizontal spread while worsening vertical placement or close approaches. Each structural proposal therefore requires coupled command fitting and a fresh feasibility assessment. The bounded campaigns establish best-found solutions under their recorded budgets, not physical impossibility of rejected structures or global optimality of accepted ones.

\subsection{Physical and numerical limits}
The imposed field, spherical primary-droplet representation and aerodynamic closures reduce the repeated computation. The model does not resolve breakup, liquid deformation or the carrier-fluid response through a new two-way CFD solve for each candidate. Positive-air extensions are implemented, but mean positive-air flow is zero in the reported application studies.

Euler/RK4 and timestep comparisons assess selected numerical trajectories at fixed release times. Their finest RK4 result is a numerical reference rather than an exact solution. Grid-boundary events, discrete wake-source changes and restricted contact eligibility remain part of the model. Clearances are reported using the specified center-coordinate diagnostics; they do not substitute for a complete solid-intersection and nonprinting-droplet collection model.

The two application configurations retain different droplet-property records, so their comparison does not isolate firing structure as the sole cause of different errors. The preceding/current reference also excludes older and following emissions. Transfer to a different nozzle, geometry, ink or history horizon requires its own model configuration and assessment.

\subsection{Confidentiality and reproducible scope}
The public experimental evidence is the released coordinate averages and their fixed simulation counterparts. Raw lab images, replicate-level coordinates and further acquisition details remain confidential. The repository supports reproduction of the reported residuals and coordinate reconstructions from those averages; independent reconstruction of the original metrology and estimation of replicate uncertainty are outside that released scope.

\subsection{Development value and next steps}
The immediate development output is a reusable package of physical inputs, context coverage, controller commands, replay results and measured-coordinate residuals. This supports systematic digital evaluation between physical characterization and final printer qualification. A prospective comparison of engineer-hours, rig-hours, computation and consumables would be needed to quantify department-level savings.

Further work can address longer continuous-message histories, automated guard-count growth and transfer to new devices. Those extensions are distinct from the implemented fixed-budget searches reported here. The forward model also provides a reference for future learned surrogates, whose accuracy and optimized commands would require their own assessment.

The high-speed application has larger residuals in both coordinate directions; the cases retain different operating properties, so this difference is not attributed solely to raster structure.
